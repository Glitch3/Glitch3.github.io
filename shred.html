Hit the jumps, grind the rails, dodge the trees.
Land your tricks straight to score.

Land facing forwards or backwards, and the right way up. Spins land on every half turn, flips on every full turn. Landing clean tricks in a row builds a combo multiplier. Needs a keyboard.
