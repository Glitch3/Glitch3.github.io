Jump
Score: 0 | Best: 0

Bounce from platform to platform and climb as high as you can. Steer with the arrow keys or A and D, and shoot upwards with the up arrow or W. On a phone, hold the left or right side of the board to steer, and tap the top of the board to shoot. Press Space or Start to play.

  • Solid platforms are safe. Platforms with a white line move. Outlined ones crumble when you land on them. Dashed ones vanish after one bounce.
  • Monsters appear as you climb. Jump on their heads or shoot them for 50 points. Touching them any other way knocks you out.
  • Avoid the holes. They pull you in if you get close.
  • Springs give a big jump. Rockets fly you straight up. Shields protect you from monsters and holes for a few seconds.
